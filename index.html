Our story

The passion for pizza.

Pizzeria Classico has been family owned since 1985 on Sutter St. in Folsom. Our family has opened 15 restaurants since 1981 and still owns Il Forno Classico in Gold River.

We use fresh dough and offer many styles: thin, hand-tossed, stuffed double crust, deep dish and calzones. Our tomato products are sourced locally and our mozzarella is arguably the best we can buy. Sit down in a comfortable booth or grab it to go.
